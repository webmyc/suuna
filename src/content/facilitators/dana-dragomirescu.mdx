# Dana Dragomirescu

Dana Dragomirescu is a trainer and counselor in relationships and communication through the ESPERE® Method, a classical psychodrama director and trainer-in-training.

## Background

As a woman, wife, mother, and creator, Dana facilitates workshops and personal development programs. Her mission is to bring practices that support the being as a whole through sensory reconnection, body awareness, and the integration of healthy emotional and relational ecology.

## Approach

Through the ESPERE® Method and psychodrama techniques, Dana helps individuals and groups develop healthier communication patterns and emotional awareness.
